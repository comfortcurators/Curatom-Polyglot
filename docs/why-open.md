# Why Curatom Polyglot is open source

Curatom mediates authority given by a human to software. Its important claims are therefore not only product claims; they are claims about where authority originates, what crosses a trust boundary, when authority is consumed, and what evidence remains afterward.

Those claims should be inspectable in code.

That is why Curatom Polyglot is published under `AGPL-3.0-only`. The license permits use, study, modification and redistribution while requiring the corresponding source of modified network-served versions to remain available under the license's terms. For a system whose enforcement boundary can itself be operated as network software, that property is intentional rather than incidental.

Open source does not mean every deployment is identical, every external service is part of this repository, or every unfinished mechanism is represented as finished. `DEFERRED.md` exists precisely to keep those distinctions visible. The architecture documentation separates implemented behavior from design questions, and the release process records what was actually verified.

Comfort Curators may operate services, sell support, or offer separate commercial terms where it has the legal rights to do so. None of those activities changes the license granted on the code published here.

The practical commitment is simple: if Curatom asks people to trust a mechanism that governs machine authority, the mechanism published here should be available for them to inspect rather than requiring trust in a diagram or a hosted black box.
